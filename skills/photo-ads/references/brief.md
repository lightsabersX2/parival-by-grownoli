# Brief

A brief is the few answers an ad cannot be made without when the brand's site does not hold them. It is one round of questions, then the ad.

Find the facts yourself first: the site, the files the person gave you, the brand's `brand.md`. Facts are yours to look up. Decisions are the person's.

Ask every open question in one message, numbered, each with the answer you propose from what you found, so the person can reply "yes to all" or correct one number. When the person hands over a filled brief in any form, read the answers from it and ask only what it leaves open.

1. **Goal.** The one action the ad asks for: buy, book, join, give, sign up, visit, or remember the name.
2. **Audience.** Who should stop for this ad, described by their situation, and who it is not for. An older audience renders with `--older`.
3. **Offer.** What exactly the person gets, the price or terms, and why now. A date or a limit goes in the ad only when it is real.
4. **Proof.** The real thing that supports the offer: a figure, a review, press, a true before and after. "None" is a full answer, and the ad then carries no proof.
5. **Where it leads.** The page the ad points to, and the words of the action.
6. **Where it runs.** The channels, which set the sizes. Grownoli Studio opens 1:1, 3:4, 9:16, 16:9 and 4:3. One ad defaults to 1:1.
7. **Pictures.** The person's own pictures to use, and whether real stock photos are acceptable.
8. **Must and must not.** Wording that has to appear, legal lines, a seal, words the brand avoids.

**A brand with no site, or a made-up brand,** also needs its look: the name, a logo if one exists, colours and a typeface. Propose a look when the person has none, and record the agreed one in `brand.md`. For a made-up brand, drop question 4 and say in `notes.md` that the brand and its facts are invented.

Budget, production dates, approvers and tracking belong to the campaign, and the ad is made without them.

Done when every question has the person's answer or their yes to your proposal. Save the answers as `brief.md` in the ad's folder, list any answer you supplied yourself under what to approve in `notes.md`, then start at step 1.

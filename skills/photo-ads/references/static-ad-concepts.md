# Static ad rules and concepts

Kept from the archived generic skill `social-media-design-performance`. A paid social ad needs a concept, not only a good design.

## Static ad rules

- One concept per ad.
- One primary hook.
- Big headline readable within 1 second.
- Product visible unless the concept intentionally delays reveal.
- Clear visual hierarchy: hook -> product or scene -> proof -> CTA.
- Text must be readable on mobile without zooming.
- Use emotional payoff, outcome, or proof to stop attention.
- Keep the design uncluttered.
- Respect platform safe zones.
- Use contrast to create scroll-stopping clarity.
- Do not bury the offer.
- Do not use claims that cannot be backed.

## Static ad concept library

Use these as starting points, then adapt them to the brand.

| Concept | Use when | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Native phone mimicry | The brand wants disarming, scroll-native creative | Screenshot-style layout, short note, product benefit, CTA |
| Absurd product scene | The feed feels predictable | Surreal visual, simple headline, product as hero |
| Data proof | There is strong survey or performance proof | Big stat, product image, benefit, proof note |
| Reverse psychology | The category is saturated | Negative or contrarian headline, product reveal, benefit |
| Direct headliner | Warm audiences need clarity | Big benefit headline, 3 bullets, product, CTA |
| Comparison | The product beats a known alternative | Them vs us, visual proof, switching reason |
| Testimonial quote | Review language is strong | Real customer line, product, benefit, CTA |
| Problem solution | The pain is obvious | Problem headline, product as fix, outcome |
| Before after | The result is visible and compliant | Before state, after state, timeline, proof note |
| Offer closer | Audience is most aware | Offer, deadline or bundle, risk reversal, CTA |
